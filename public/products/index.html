How the Optima Product Model Works

One technology organization. Multiple product lines. Configured per business.

01

Products, not rebuilds

Optima builds reusable digital products — each deployed to many clients with configuration, not a custom rebuild each time.

02

Isolated tenants

Each organization gets its own data, users, and configuration — without a separate codebase or infrastructure.

03

Dedicated product brands

When a product reaches sufficient scale, it gets its own dedicated brand — like CreditRiskDynamics for CAP.

04

Enterprise when needed

If a product doesn't fit, Optima builds a purpose-built enterprise platform shaped around your specific operation.
