What it covers

Automation, integration, dashboards, and digital workflows.

The full scope behind the Digital Transformation card — modernizing how the business actually operates day to day.

Automation

Remove repetitive handoffs — notifications, assignments, calculations, and status updates that should not need a person.

System integration

Connect finance, ops, CRM, or legacy tools so data moves once — not copied into three places.

Manual-process digitization

Operational dashboards
